About TukuyBooks

TukuyBooks is an open-source initiative to scrape, process, and convert freely available online documentation into high-quality, offline-accessible EPUBs and PDFs. Our mission is to preserve and democratize knowledge from the open-source internet, making it available to anyone, anywhere, without barriers.

Web Scraping

Capture valuable documentation from open-source websites with custom spiders.

Ebook Generation

Transform web content into portable, optimized formats (EPUB, PDF) with proper formatting.

Free Distribution

Download and share these resources freely via our platform and GitHub.
